Complete Guide for Foreigners Starting a Hotel Business in Japan

Introduction

In recent years, inquiries from China, Taiwan, Hong Kong, and Singapore about "wanting to operate a hotel or accommodation facility in Japan" have been increasing. Even foreigners can operate accommodation businesses in Japan. This article explains the steps for foreigners to start a hotel business in Japan.

Step 1: Company Establishment

Generally, you will establish a Japanese corporation. Options include Kabushiki Kaisha (stock company) and Godo Kaisha (LLC). It is important to choose the appropriate corporate form based on business scale, credibility needs, and future expansion plans.

Step 2: Property Selection

Key factors in property selection include distance from the station, tourism demand, zoning regulations, and fire safety requirements. Do not judge solely by price. It is essential to verify in advance whether the property can obtain a hotel business license.

Step 3: Obtaining a Hotel Business License

Compliance with health center, fire department, and building standards is required. For foreigners, dealing with Japanese documentation and communication with relevant authorities can be challenging. Professional support is recommended.

Step 4: Building Operations

OTA (booking platform) registration, cleaning systems, review management, and multilingual support are necessary. As a foreign owner, it is especially important to establish systems for handling inquiries in Japanese and communicating with neighboring residents.

Common Mistakes

Purchasing a property that cannot obtain a license. Inadequate management systems leading to low occupancy rates. Weak financial plans with unexpected costs. Thorough advance research and expert advice are essential to avoid these pitfalls.
