FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Can a foreigner incorporate a company in Japan?

Yes, it is possible. Even if you do not reside in Japan, you can establish a Japanese company. Kaisei will support you through the process.

Q. Can I invest without living in Japan?

Yes, you can. We support everything from property research to contract signing without you needing to be on-site. Online communication is also available.

Q. What is the difference between minpaku and inns/hotel licensing?

Minpaku is governed by the Housing Lodging Business Act, while inns and hotels are governed by the Inns & Hotels Act. The legal framework and permit requirements differ. Kaisei will recommend the best approach for you.

Q. Can you handle operations management as well?

Yes, we also provide post-launch operational support. See our Hospitality Support page for details on operational management and improvement services.
