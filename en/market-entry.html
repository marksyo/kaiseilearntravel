FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

We are an overseas company. Can we request your services even if we don't speak Japanese?▼

Yes, we can help. We support communication in Chinese and English. If Japanese-language local support is needed, our company will provide interpretation and translation services.

What type of contract will be used?▼

Our standard approach is a service agreement. We can also discuss performance-based contracts. Please tell us your requirements during a free consultation.

How do you find PoC pilot sites?▼

We select optimal pilot sites from our network. After the PoC, feedback from the pilot sites can be used for product improvement.

Is it necessary to establish a Japanese entity to enter the Japanese market?▼

Not necessarily. A common approach is to first proceed with market research and PoC through a service agreement with us, then consider incorporation after building a track record.
