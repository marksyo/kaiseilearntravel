President's Message

When I first came to Japan, I faced many challenges: finding a place to live, language barriers, cultural differences, my children's education, and searching for a job. Going through those experiences firsthand is what led me to want to help others facing the same difficulties.

I started by offering free consultations on children's education. Hearing so many people say "that really helped me" became the driving force behind everything I do today.

Japan faces an aging population with a slowing economy. In these times, I believe every foreigner who comes to Japan should have the opportunity to contribute and thrive. That belief led me to start the hospitality business.

Today, our work has expanded to supporting overseas real estate investment and helping Chinese manufacturers in the accommodation sector enter the Japanese market.

"Live in Japan, grow your business" — I am committed to supporting you with all my heart.

Li Yan, Representative Director
