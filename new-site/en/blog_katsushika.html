Benefits of Starting Accommodation in Katsushika Ward

PHOTO: Replace with appropriate image

Katsushika Ward, located in eastern Tokyo, is often overlooked by accommodation investors who focus on central wards. However, this traditional downtown district (shitamachi) offers compelling advantages for those willing to look beyond the obvious choices. From lower property costs to rich cultural heritage, Katsushika presents a unique opportunity in Tokyo's accommodation market.

Lower Property Costs, Higher Margins

Property acquisition costs in Katsushika are significantly lower than in central wards such as Shinjuku, Shibuya, or even Taito. This means a lower initial investment and potentially higher profit margins once operations begin. For first-time accommodation investors or those with limited capital, Katsushika offers a more accessible entry point into Tokyo's lucrative tourism market.

Excellent Access to Major Tourism Hubs

Despite its location in eastern Tokyo, Katsushika offers convenient access to major tourism destinations. Asakusa and Ueno are within 20-30 minutes by train, making it easy to attract guests who want affordable accommodation near — but not in — the busiest tourist zones. The Keisei Line also provides direct access to Narita Airport, a significant advantage for international travelers.

Traditional Downtown Culture and Shibamata

Katsushika is home to Shibamata, one of Tokyo's most charming traditional neighborhoods. Known for the Taishakuten Temple, the nostalgic Shibamata Taishakuten Approach, and the famous movie series "Otoko wa Tsurai yo," Shibamata attracts both domestic and increasingly international visitors seeking an authentic shitamachi experience. This cultural asset gives accommodation in Katsushika a unique selling point that central wards cannot replicate.

Growing Foreign Visitor Interest

While Katsushika has traditionally drawn mostly domestic tourists, international visitor numbers are growing steadily. The ward's authentic Japanese atmosphere, reasonable prices, and accessibility from Narita Airport make it increasingly attractive to foreign travelers looking for experiences beyond the typical tourist circuit. Early movers in this market stand to benefit from this growth trajectory.
