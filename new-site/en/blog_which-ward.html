Which Ward Should You Choose to Start Accommodation in Tokyo?

PHOTO: Replace with appropriate image

Tokyo's 23 wards offer vastly different environments for accommodation businesses. Choosing the right ward is one of the most critical decisions you will make — it affects property costs, guest demand, regulatory requirements, and ultimately your profitability. This article compares five key wards to help you make an informed decision.

Shinjuku and Shibuya: High Demand, High Competition

Shinjuku and Shibuya are Tokyo's premier commercial and entertainment hubs. They attract massive visitor volumes — both domestic and international — and command the highest average daily rates (ADR) in the city. However, property acquisition costs are extremely high, and competition among accommodation operators is intense. These wards are best suited for investors with substantial capital who can differentiate through quality and service.

Taito and Sumida: Tourism Powerhouses

Taito Ward (home to Asakusa and Ueno) and Sumida Ward (home to Tokyo Skytree) are at the heart of Tokyo's tourism district. They draw millions of visitors annually to cultural landmarks, temples, and traditional streetscapes. Accommodation supply is high but so is demand. Property costs are more moderate than Shinjuku and Shibuya, and both wards offer area cards (chiku card) that allow extended private lodging operating days. These wards are excellent choices for mid-range and budget accommodation focused on leisure travelers.

Toshima: Transportation Hub Advantage

Toshima Ward, centered on Ikebukuro Station, offers outstanding transportation access — one of the busiest railway hubs in the world. The ward has seen significant commercial redevelopment in recent years, boosting accommodation demand from both business and leisure travelers. Toshima has introduced its own minpaku regulations, so operators must ensure compliance. Property costs are generally lower than Shinjuku and Shibuya, offering a good balance of accessibility and affordability.

Making Your Choice

There is no single "best" ward — the right choice depends on your budget, target guest profile, operating model, and risk tolerance. We recommend analyzing occupancy trends, regulatory environment, and property availability in your shortlisted wards before committing.
